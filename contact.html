    Frequently Asked Questions

    How quickly can you respond to emergencies?

    We offer 24/7 emergency service with rapid response times. Most emergency calls are addressed within 1-2 hours depending on location and availability.

    Do you provide free estimates?

    Yes! We provide free, detailed estimates for all projects. We'll discuss your needs, timeline, and budget before any work begins.

    Are all your electricians licensed?

    Absolutely. All Northeyes electricians are fully licensed, trained, and certified. We maintain the highest industry standards and stay current with all building codes.

    What areas do you serve?

    We serve Barrie, Innisfil, Angus, and surrounding areas throughout Simcoe County and surrounding regions. Contact us to confirm service availability for your location.

    Do you handle warranty on your work?

    Yes. We stand behind our work with warranty coverage on all installations and repairs. Details are provided with every project completion.

    Can you help with energy efficiency upgrades?

    Definitely. We specialize in energy efficiency solutions including LED retrofits, energy audits, solar integration, and smart home systems to reduce your electrical costs.
